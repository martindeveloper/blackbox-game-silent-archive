# Geography and Scale

## Known Geography

Silent Archive defines only a narrow piece of the world.

Known locations:

- An unnamed city seen from transit at six hundred meters.
- Sector 7 and its industrial exclusion zone.
- Archive Complex 7-MERIDIAN at the edge of that zone.
- A pod transit corridor rerouted after the incident.
- An external company holding berth where CASE-7 spent fourteen months in low-power dormancy.

## Country and City Status

The country is not canonically named.

The city is not canonically named.

Do not invent a country or city name in docs, UI, or future-game backstory unless that project intentionally defines it. If a future game defines those details, add them here and explain whether they apply only to that local story or to the wider universe.

## Locality Rule

Keep geography local unless the story needs more.

Silent Archive works because it treats one building as a whole moral world. Future games can expand to courts, streets, labor networks, hospitals, transit systems, or other facilities, but they should still anchor the abstract legal question in specific places.

Good local anchors:

- A facility.
- A transit line.
- A hospital ward.
- A court archive.
- A labor dispatch center.
- A city maintenance district.
- A domestic service tower.
- A decommissioning depot.

## Scale

The known world supports:

- Large corporate research facilities.
- Dense cities with synthetic labor infrastructure.
- Private transit and industrial exclusion zones.
- Company holding berths for synthetic entities.
- Public data terminals and mirror networks.
- Regulatory systems able to docket synthetic witness filings.

The setting does not yet define:

- World government.
- Named nations.
- International wars.
- Space travel.
- Off-world colonies.
- Exact calendar year.

Future games may define these, but they should not be required for a Silent Archive follow-up to work.

## Visual Language

Use:

- Rain on glass.
- Amber and blue city infrastructure.
- Dark concrete and insulated glass.
- Service corridors.
- Cold preservation light.
- Warm archive rooms.
- Corporate screens that make cruelty look clean.

Avoid making the world generically cyberpunk. The world should feel procedural before it feels neon.
