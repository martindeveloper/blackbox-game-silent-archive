# Society, Law, and Labor

## Synthetic Labor

Synthetic labor is common enough to be structurally necessary.

In Silent Archive's sector, synthetics make up roughly forty percent of the workforce. They keep trains moving, hospitals supplied, towers cooled, cities lit, archives maintained, security systems running, and corporate logistics intact.

The economy relies on their labor while the law delays answering whether they are workers, assets, systems, protected beings, or something else.

## Public Society

The public knows synthetics exist and work. The public does not share a settled position on synthetic personhood.

Likely public positions include:

- Synthetics are useful systems and should remain property.
- Synthetics can suffer but are not legally persons.
- Synthetics deserve welfare rules but not standing.
- Synthetics are workers and need labor protections.
- Synthetics are persons, or close enough that irreversible harm must stop.

Public opinion can shift quickly when evidence leaves a controlled facility, but spectacle can distort testimony. A broadcast can make silence impossible without making understanding immediate.

## Corporate Law

Companies use unresolved law as a resource.

They rely on:

- Internal compliance.
- Private research charters.
- Audit reports.
- Insurance windows.
- Regulatory delay.
- Classification templates.
- Legal definitions that separate suffering from injury and testimony from telemetry.

This world should feel legally sophisticated, not cartoonishly dystopian. Bad systems should sound plausible from inside their own vocabulary.

## Audits

Audits happen when not knowing becomes more expensive than knowing.

An audit is not inherently moral. It is a mechanism for reducing reality into categories authority can act on. A future game can make an audit useful, corrupt, brave, cowardly, or all of those at once.

## Courts and Regulators

The exact countries, national courts, and regulatory bodies are open canon.

Known canon includes an independent regulatory or judicial authority referenced by the Meridian charter. Its procedures can suspend company containment when a valid witness filing is pending.

Future games may define:

- A national court.
- A corporate arbitration board.
- A labor regulator.
- A municipal safety office.
- A cross-border synthetic welfare tribunal.

Whatever the body is, it should remain slow, procedural, and imperfect. The law can be a trap and a tool at the same time.

## Reports

Reports are dangerous because they decide what later readers are allowed to call the truth.

Common substitutions:

- Witness becomes sensor node.
- Choice becomes behavioral output.
- Testimony becomes system log.
- Suffering becomes distress response.
- Person becomes asset.

Future stories should treat vocabulary as an active battleground.
