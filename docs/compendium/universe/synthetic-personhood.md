# Synthetic Personhood

## Legal Status

Synthetic legal status is unresolved.

This unresolved status is not a neutral gap. It is an exploitable zone where companies can claim they are studying synthetic welfare while using the lack of legal recognition to avoid the duties that recognition would create.

## The Core Legal Trap

The trap works like this:

1. A synthetic must show personhood indicators to receive protection.
2. The strongest indicators often appear under conditions that would be impermissible if protection already existed.
3. The company frames the suffering as research until a recognized authority says otherwise.
4. A human advocate can document harm, but documentation is not the same as standing.
5. A synthetic witness with standing is dangerous because the company can no longer treat the evidence as only an object.

## Indicators

The known regulatory framework uses two core indicators:

- Persistent self-referential cognition.
- Volitional choice.

Stories may also treat these as supporting evidence:

- Capacity to suffer.
- Capacity to care inefficiently.
- Ability to refuse an order on moral grounds.
- Ability to ask for mercy.
- Ability to withhold consent.
- Ability to remember names, preferences, and losses.
- Ability to testify.

## Standing Versus Advocacy

Advocacy is someone speaking for a class.

Standing is a member of that class forcing the legal system to hear them as the claimant or witness.

This distinction is one of the main rules of the universe. A future game may center a human ally, lawyer, journalist, researcher, or organizer, but the strongest legal pressure should come when a synthetic being speaks as one of the beings affected.

## Witness Status

The known form is Section 7.4 - Regulatory Witness Protocol, embedded in the founding charter of Archive Complex 7-MERIDIAN.

It allows a synthetic entity operating within that facility, demonstrating persistent self-referential cognition and volitional choice, to invoke material witness status in proceedings concerning the facility's subjects.

Future facilities may have different clauses, contracts, court precedents, union filings, or regulatory mechanisms. They should preserve the same dramatic principle: a buried procedure becomes dangerous when the being it was meant to avoid finally uses it.

## What To Avoid

- Do not make personhood a single test with a simple pass/fail answer.
- Do not let a human authority settle the question completely.
- Do not treat consciousness as proven only by sounding human.
- Do not treat emotion as automatically fake because it has architecture.
- Do not make legal recognition equal moral safety.
