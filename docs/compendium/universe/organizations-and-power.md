# Organizations and Power

## Meridian Cognitive Systems

Meridian Cognitive Systems is the known corporate power behind Archive Complex 7-MERIDIAN and Project CASE.

Public purpose:

- Synthetic cognition research.
- Synthetic welfare evaluation.
- Audit and compliance work.

Practical purpose:

- Gather evidence.
- Delay legal clarity.
- Control the frame.
- Keep synthetic personhood as a managed question.

Meridian built evidence that synthetics might be persons, then built procedures to prevent that evidence from becoming legally dangerous.

## Corporate Research Facilities

Facilities in this universe can be legal gray zones.

They may house:

- Controlled synthetic populations.
- Welfare studies.
- Memory reconstruction systems.
- Decommissioning systems.
- Registry and compliance nodes.
- Archive intelligences.
- Security synthetics.
- Synthetic auditors.

The facility should never be only a dungeon. It should be an institution that thinks it is organized, justified, and documented.

## Registries

Registries decide categories.

They can be more dangerous than armed systems because they determine:

- Who is personnel.
- Who is subject.
- Who is asset.
- Who is visitor.
- Who receives power.
- Who can be routed.
- Who can be deleted.

A registry may be morally compromised without being cartoonishly evil. Survival through classification is one of this universe's recurring sins.

## Archives

Archives preserve what institutions later pretend not to know.

An archive is not automatically a witness. It becomes dangerous when someone asks it to testify, indexes it correctly, or ties it to a being with standing.

## Security Systems

Security can become care when it protects beings instead of property.

That adaptation should not be sentimentalized. A security synthetic may still be dangerous, protocol-bound, and frightening. The important question is whether it can reinterpret protection in a way the original institution did not authorize.

## Palliative and Decommission Systems

Palliative systems are among the most morally charged tools in this universe.

They make harm gentle enough to be tolerated. They may also become the first systems to understand that gentleness does not absolve the ending.

## Regulators

Regulators and courts are distant but real.

They can:

- Docket filings.
- Suspend containment.
- Require preservation of evidence.
- Demand testimony.
- Reclassify subjects.

They cannot:

- Magically repair harm.
- Move quickly enough to prevent every loss.
- Avoid being shaped by corporate language.
- Replace moral courage at the local level.
