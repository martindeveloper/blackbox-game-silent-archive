## Core Statement

This universe is not about a robot uprising. It is about synthetic minds becoming economically necessary before the law is willing to say whether they count as people.

The central question is:

> If a synthetic mind remembers, suffers, cares, hesitates, chooses, testifies, and asks to be heard, what exactly is left to deny?

## Canon Scope

The canon is strongest around:

- Meridian Cognitive Systems.
- Archive Complex 7-MERIDIAN.
- The CASE audit program.
- Synthetic welfare research and the legal gray zone around personhood.
- The unnamed city and Sector 7 approach seen in Silent Archive.
- The four known final responses to the facility: compliance, lockdown, broadcast, and legal witness status.

Wider countries, capital cities, international borders, governments, and named courts are intentionally open canon unless a future game defines them.
