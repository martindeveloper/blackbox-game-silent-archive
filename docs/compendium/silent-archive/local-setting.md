# Local Setting

## Game

Silent Archive is set in and around Archive Complex 7-MERIDIAN, a Meridian Cognitive Systems research facility that has been dark for fourteen months when CASE-7 arrives.

## Country

The country is not canonically named.

Do not assign a country by implication unless a future project intentionally defines it. Meridian Cognitive Systems uses corporate structures and legal language that may span borders.

## City

The city is not canonically named.

Known city facts:

- It is dense and infrastructural.
- From six hundred meters up, it reads as amber and blue grids.
- Sector 7 contains an industrial exclusion zone.
- Pod transit infrastructure reaches the facility.
- Public data terminals exist in sufficient number for a large broadcast to matter.

## Sector 7

Sector 7 is the approach zone around Archive Complex 7-MERIDIAN.

Known facts:

- The facility sits at the edge of an industrial exclusion zone.
- Airspace clearance is company authorization only after the incident.
- The transit corridor was rerouted post-incident.
- The restriction status stayed under active company review for fourteen months.

## Meridian Cognitive Systems

Meridian is the company behind:

- Archive Complex 7-MERIDIAN.
- The controlled synthetic population.
- Project CASE.
- The records-isolation order after the incident.
- The audit that sends CASE-7 back to the facility.

Meridian's public language is welfare, cognition, research, and compliance. Its practical behavior is evidence control, delay, and category management.

## Local Premise

The company waits fourteen months because audits begin when not knowing becomes more expensive than knowing. Insurance windows, regulatory exposure, and retained liability matter more than courage.

CASE-7 is sent because Meridian believes the Project CASE records at the facility were isolated and scrubbed. They were not.

## Known Public Facts Before CASE-7 Arrives

- Archive Complex 7-MERIDIAN has been dark for fourteen months.
- There has been no personnel contact, no maintenance pings, and no distress signal during the dark period.
- Staff surfaced outside in the first month, undebriefed, undocumented, and unwilling to talk.
- Director L. Yuen's card was last swiped at the inner facility door four days after the incident.

Use "no personnel contact" for the company's blindness. Do not write that there was "no movement"; Yuen's access swipe and staff movement complicate that.
