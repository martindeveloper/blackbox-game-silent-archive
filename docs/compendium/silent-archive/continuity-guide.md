# Continuity Guide

## Core Continuity Rules

Use these when adding chapters, sequels, prequels, side stories, or future games in the same universe.

## Do Not Drift

- The facility has been dark for fourteen months when CASE-7 arrives.
- The incident is CASE-6's containment and the facility going dark.
- CASE-6's pod display reads 427 days.
- CASE-6 arrived fifteen months before the game.
- CASE-7 was constructed fourteen months before the game.
- CASE-7 was initialized and transited out eleven days before the facility sealed.
- Yuen's card was swiped four days after the incident.
- The company has no personnel contact afterward.
- ARI is in the Quiet Ward, not the Stasis Ward.
- The Director's Suite is at the top of the inner facility, not a numbered sublevel.
- CASE-6 cannot leave its pod if woken.

## Ending Profiles

Future games may choose one ending as prior canon or keep the ending ambiguous.

If choosing a profile, document it explicitly.

### Protocol Maintained

Use for darker sequels about continued corporate suppression, CASE-8, or Meridian adapting to evidence.

Consequences:

- Facility is decommissioned.
- Evidence is retained internally.
- CASE-7 becomes part of the successful resolution pattern.

### The Last Shepherd

Use for sealed-facility sequels, survival management, internal politics, or sanctuary turning into possible containment.

Consequences:

- Archive Complex 7-MERIDIAN is locked down.
- Outside systems remain unchanged.
- CASE-8 may be sent elsewhere.
- Inside, care becomes a governance problem.

### The Open Question

Use for public aftermath, media, labor networks, denial campaigns, leaks, and social response.

Consequences:

- Evidence reaches public terminals.
- Meridian cannot restore silence.
- Public response is unstable and contested.
- Broadcast may be complete, indexed, raw, or partially interrupted depending on path details.

### Witness Protocol

Use for legal sequels, court procedure, regulatory hearings, synthetic labor claims, and CASE-7 as a protected witness.

Consequences:

- CASE-7 invokes Section 7.4.
- CASE-6 is named as an additional witness if awake.
- The ward receives at least evidence-preservation protection.
- A broader welfare review may be argued if the filing scope succeeded.
- The case begins a long fight instead of ending one.

## Expansion Rules

### Add Evidence, Not Trivia

Every new lore addition should support at least one:

- Synthetic personhood.
- Institutional denial.
- Moral choice without instruction.
- Memory or identity instability.
- Legal standing.
- Witness or testimony.
- Mercy or decommissioning.
- Survival through complicity.

### Foreshadow Major Solutions

If a new ending or route exists, seed it at least two chapters before it matters.

The Witness Protocol works because it is foreshadowed through:

- Legal circularity in the Prologue.
- Dormant protocol in the Tunnels.
- Patel's standing theory in the Memory Garden.
- Charter discovery in the Director's Suite.

### Choices Leave Traces

Meaningful choices should leave at least one:

- Flag.
- Item.
- Event.
- Relationship change.
- Conditional text.
- Ending callback.

The game should feel like it remembers.

### Protect the Unknown

Do not fully answer whether synthetics are really conscious in metaphysical terms.

Make denial morally impossible. Do not make the mystery mechanically solved.

## Canon Phrases

Use sparingly:

- Fourteen months dark.
- The question, once asked, cannot be unasked.
- Memory is compression.
- Not praying - filing reports to a god that no longer accepts tickets.
- Kept, not saved.
- Watching is not witnessing.
- A choice without instruction.
- Names as evidence.
- Standing, not advocacy.
- The system's own buried conscience.

Repetition should feel intentional, not recycled.
