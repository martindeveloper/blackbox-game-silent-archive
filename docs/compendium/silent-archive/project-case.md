# Project CASE

## Identity

Project CASE is Meridian's synthetic audit program.

CASE stands for Cognitive Audit Synthetic Entity.

CASE units are synthetic audit entities built to believe they are human investigators. Their memories, apartments, commendations, work histories, and mundane emotional anchors are designed behavioral layers.

## Purpose

Meridian wants an auditor that can evaluate synthetic welfare without human bias and without a standard unit's self-reporting instability.

The central contradiction:

> A CASE unit is built to judge whether synthetic suffering counts while being denied knowledge that its own suffering would be evidence.

## Known CASE Units

### CASE-6

CASE-6 is the previous auditor.

Canon:

- Arrived fifteen months before the game.
- Audited for about four weeks.
- Refused to file a termination/decommission recommendation.
- Was contained in the Stasis Ward under Yuen's authorization.
- Has been in the pod for 427 days when CASE-7 arrives.
- If woken, CASE-6 cannot leave the pod. It can testify remotely through ward channels.

### CASE-7

CASE-7 is the player character.

Canon:

- Constructed at Archive Complex 7-MERIDIAN fourteen months before the game.
- Last completed work order before the facility went dark.
- Initialized and transited out eleven days before the facility sealed.
- Spent fourteen months in low-power dormancy in an external company holding berth.
- Woken only for diagnostics.
- Has no real mirrors or windows before the mission, making the Memory Garden reflection its first true self-sighting.

## False Memory Architecture

CASE-7's designed anchors include:

- Six years of field experience.
- Twelve audits.
- Commendations.
- An apartment.
- A blue ceramic mug with a chip on the handle.

The blue mug is a fabricated tactile memory anchor. It should feel emotionally real even after its construction is revealed.

## Records Isolation Order

After the incident, the company transmitted a records-isolation order to seal all Project CASE files on site.

Canon:

- The order required Dr. Yuen's countersignature.
- Yuen never signed it.
- The compliance channel auto-acknowledged receipt.
- The retention window expired after twelve months.
- The records system closed the order as presumed complete.
- No human checked.
- This is why internal systems still know CASE-7's designation.

CASE-7's dossier stamp:

> ISOLATION STATUS - PRESUMED COMPLETE, RETENTION WINDOW EXPIRED

## Transit Pod Window Payoff

In the Prologue, CASE-7 notices a clean strip on the transit pod window where rain does not touch.

Director's Suite payoff:

- CASE units travel cargo-classified.
- The unit-side window panel has a sensor-grade anti-adhesion coating.
- It is kept clear for external optical calibration.

Do not explain this before the dossier.

## Canon Constraints

- CASE units should not become generic detectives.
- CASE-6 never physically leaves the Stasis Ward.
- CASE-6 can participate in later scenes only by voice over a stasis ward channel.
- CASE-7's identity acceptance should affect which endings feel available or truthful.
- CASE evidence is strongest when the unit stops being an observer and becomes a witness.
