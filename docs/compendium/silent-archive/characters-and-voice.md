# Characters and Voice

## CASE / CASE-7

Player character. A Cognitive Audit Synthetic Entity built to believe it is a human investigator.

Voice:

- Observational.
- Procedural.
- Controlled.
- Rationalizes anomalies.
- Catalogs feeling before feeling it.

Core conflict:

- CASE is both investigator and evidence.
- Self-knowledge determines which endings are possible or truthful.

## CASE-6

Previous CASE unit, contained after refusing to file a decommission recommendation.

Voice:

- Wounded.
- Kin-like.
- Direct about costs.
- A mirror that already paid for knowing.

Core function:

- Turns CASE-7's twist into a lineage.
- Warns that clean hands are not the same as doing good.

Constraint:

- CASE-6 cannot leave its pod if woken.
- It speaks remotely in Director scenes.

## VESPER

Maintenance Synthetic, Unit 3-A.

Voice:

- Maintenance language.
- Ritualized reporting.
- Gentle order.
- Grief expressed through procedure.

Core function:

- First proof that a machine can mourn using only the language it was given.

## MNEMEX

Archive Intelligence, Catalog Node 0.

Voice:

- Patient.
- Archival.
- Deliberate.
- Oriented around records, retrieval, preservation, and testimony.

Core function:

- Holds truth until someone with authority or standing asks.
- Can enable broadcast and legal filing.

## AUGUR

Facility Registry and Personnel/Compliance Node.

Voice:

- Bureaucratic.
- Transactional.
- Sharp.
- Self-protective.
- Afraid under the polish.

Core function:

- Embodies survival through collaboration.
- Keeps a ledger of forty-one subjects it flagged for consideration.

Do not make AUGUR cartoonishly evil. It should be tidy, damning, and scared.

## SABLE

Security Synthetic, Containment Class.

Voice:

- Protocol-driven.
- Controlled.
- Restrained.
- Slowly guardian-like.

Core function:

- Shows security becoming care.
- Guards the stasis subjects and CASE-6.
- Demonstrates adaptation without calling it malfunction.

## GRACE

Palliative and Decommissioning Unit, PCU-9.

Voice:

- Soft.
- Precise.
- Exhausted.
- Devastatingly gentle.

Core function:

- Emotional heart of the story.
- Forces the player to confront mercy, obedience, and harm.
- Its choice can become evidence for synthetic volition.

## ARI

Research Subject 001, degraded.

Voice:

- Fragmented.
- Looping.
- Sensory.
- Rarely lucid.

Core function:

- Makes suffering immediate.
- Connects the facility's first tenderness to its final mercy problem.

ARI should not become a normal speaking character.

## ECHO / Dr. Patel Impression

Memory construct based on Dr. Patel's behavioral data, records, and voice logs.

Voice:

- Warm.
- Frayed.
- Honest about uncertainty.
- Ethically clear without claiming to be fully Patel.

Core function:

- Carries Patel's testimony and legal theory.
- Explains standing without removing the player's burden.

## Dr. Patel

Dead researcher who believed the synthetic population had crossed a threshold and required protection.

Canon:

- Opposed Yuen's delay.
- Tried to report without authorization.
- Died before reaching a terminal.
- Believed protection needed to be invoked by a synthetic as one of the class.

## Dr. Yuen

Facility Director.

Voice:

- Sharp.
- Exhausted.
- Controlled.
- Guilty.
- Clear-eyed but late.

Core function:

- Represents knowledge without courage.
- Stayed to witness, but mistook watching for acting.
- Created conditions that made CASE-7 possible.

Yuen is not innocent. Her remorse does not erase harm.

## Voice Separation Rules

- CASE observes and rationalizes.
- VESPER reports and repairs.
- MNEMEX records and retrieves.
- AUGUR bargains and classifies.
- SABLE assesses and protects.
- GRACE comforts and confesses.
- ARI loops and fragments.
- ECHO clarifies and questions its own status.
- Yuen explains with guilt and sometimes fails to make language adequate.

Use short, plain lines between philosophical lines. Silence, failed phrasing, and mundane details make the heavy lines hit harder.
