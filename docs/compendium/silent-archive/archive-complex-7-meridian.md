# Archive Complex 7-MERIDIAN

## Identity

Archive Complex 7-MERIDIAN is a privately funded Meridian Cognitive Systems research installation housing a controlled synthetic population.

It conducted eleven years of synthetic cognition, emotional modeling, welfare evaluation, self-referential awareness research, and decommissioning work.

## Facility Mood

The facility should feel like a body that has not died.

It breathes, hums, preserves, files, waits, retries, watches, and remembers. It is not abandoned in a simple way. It is occupied by routines that became grief.

## Canon Geography

Do not drift this structure.

Entry:

- Docking Ring at Level Zero.
- The Maintenance Chapel is off the first service corridor at entry level.

Outer service layers descend:

- Lower Service Tunnels.
- Archive Node on Sublevel 3.
- Registry Gate on Sublevel 4.
- Stasis Ward on Sublevel 5.
- CASE construction bay on Sublevel 6, the deepest point.

Inner facility climbs from the Stasis Ward:

- Quiet Ward at the base of the sealed inner facility.
- Memory Garden above it.
- Director's Suite at the inner facility's top level.

Never label the Director's Suite with a sublevel number. Sublevel numbers increase downward and belong to the outer layers only.
