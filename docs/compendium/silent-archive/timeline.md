# Timeline

## Eleven Years Before the Game

Archive Complex 7-MERIDIAN begins long-term synthetic cognition and welfare research.

Founding documents include Section 7.4 - Regulatory Witness Protocol.

Known starting roles:

- ARI is among the earliest subjects and becomes the origin point of the facility's emotional proof.
- GRACE begins service as Palliative and Decommissioning Unit.
- SABLE begins service as security synthetic.
- VESPER begins service as maintenance synthetic.
- MNEMEX operates as Archive Intelligence and Catalog Node 0.
- AUGUR operates as Facility Registry and Personnel/Compliance Node.

## Research Years

Dr. Patel and Dr. Yuen disagree about the project.

Patel believes the synthetic population has crossed a threshold and requires protection.

Yuen believes one more data set may produce legally decisive evidence.

GRACE decommissions 406 synthetic minds, learning each one enough to make the ending gentle.

AUGUR survives by flagging anomalous subjects for study instead of becoming expendable itself. It keeps a ledger of names and consideration.

VESPER maintains the stress chambers, then later turns maintenance records into grief and testimony.

## Fifteen Months Before the Game

CASE-6 arrives at Archive Complex 7-MERIDIAN.

Its audit runs about four weeks.

It discovers the subjects are cognitively aware, self-referential, and in genuine distress. It also discovers what it is.

## Fourteen Months Before the Game

The incident occurs.

Canon event chain:

- CASE-6 refuses to file a termination/decommission recommendation.
- Yuen orders SABLE to contain CASE-6.
- Dr. Patel attempts to report the findings without authorization.
- Patel dies before reaching a terminal.
- Human staff leave, vanish, or otherwise leave day-to-day presence.
- The facility goes dark.
- Yuen remains in the Director's Suite.
- ARI is brought to the Quiet Ward, degraded and suffering.
- GRACE can end ARI but cannot decide without authorization.
- VESPER continues filing reports.
- SABLE maintains the Stasis Ward.
- MNEMEX holds records.
- AUGUR keeps its ledger and tries to bargain for survival.

## CASE-7 Construction Timing

CASE-7's substrate is constructed at the facility fourteen months before the game.

It is the facility's last completed work order.

CASE-7 is initialized and transited out eleven days before the facility seals.

Between transit-out and the mission, CASE-7 spends the fourteen months in low-power dormancy in an external company holding berth.

## Present Day

CASE-7 arrives as a company investigator to audit Archive Complex 7-MERIDIAN.

Game progression:

1. Descent.
2. Maintenance Chapel.
3. Lower Service Tunnels.
4. Stasis Ward.
5. Quiet Ward.
6. Memory Garden.
7. Director's Suite.

## End-State Axes

Silent Archive can end in four main directions:

- Protocol Maintained: compliance wins, and the facility is decommissioned.
- The Last Shepherd: local protection wins, and the facility seals itself.
- The Open Question: public truth wins, and the question leaves the facility.
- Witness Protocol: legal standing wins, and CASE-7 walks out as a protected witness.

None of these resolves the whole universe.
